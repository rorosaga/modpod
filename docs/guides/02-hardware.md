# Install the Quad & battery

Follow the illustrated repair and manufacturer guides, with a fit check before closing the case.

## Before starting

Complete the backup and baseline first. These checkpoints accompany the full photo guides; keep those guides open during the repair.

## 1. Open with the photo guide

Follow iFixit’s opening sequence. Support both halves and the headphone ribbon as the case opens. Photograph the existing cable routing before moving parts.

## 2. Disconnect power, then replace storage

Disconnect the battery carefully before removing the HDD. The battery socket and its small latch are fragile. Follow iFlash’s ribbon-lock photos when seating the Quad; never force a ribbon or pry the whole socket off the board.

## 3. Start with one card

Insert your 128 GB microSD in uSD1. Follow the Quad instructions for card seating, ZIF engagement, and supplied foam placement. Save the original HDD and battery separately.

## 4. Dry-fit the new battery

Check the exact pack dimensions, ribbon routing, and clearance alongside the Quad. Keep the battery flat and uncompressed. If the assembly resists closing, stop and reassess the fit. Do not bend, puncture, or sand a battery. Reconnect power only after the other connections are secure.

## Checklist

- [ ] Battery disconnected before storage work
- [ ] Quad ribbon seated and card in uSD1
- [ ] Battery clearance and safe cable routing checked
- [ ] Open-case assembly photographed before testing

## Keep in mind

The Techtek pack is advertised for 5th/5.5th-gen iFlash builds. Its fit in your exact Quad + case assembly has not been confirmed. Test the assembly before snapping all the case clips shut.
